ListDetail

The one behavior of a region that shows a list beside the open item (spec §6.47; mobile layout spec §5.2), drawn with the inbox: on compact the list is the page (compact-list, a tab root with the bottom navigation) and an item is pushed full screen (compact-detail: the pushed top bar with back, the item, the decision row sticky at the bottom, no bottom navigation); on medium, when the template frame is at least 320 + 360 wide, two panes (two-pane: the list column 320 with a `line-soft` divider and the current row on the `accent-tint` ground with the 2 px accent marker, the detail at least 360).
