Sheet drag states

The follow-finger states of a bottom sheet (spec §6.7; mobile layout spec §7.4), drawn with a ListPage's filter sheet over the list: dragging (the sheet follows the finger, here 24% of its height down, with no transition; the scrim fades by the same share, the grab handle on `muted`) and settling (released past the 40% threshold, so it carries off to the dismissed position over `motion.expand`, `ease-out`; here at 60%; a release below the threshold springs back the same way). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it still drags but snaps without animation.
