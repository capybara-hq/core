CopyField

A value people copy and may see again (spec §6.44): the label, a read-only field and Copy, which reads 已复制 for two seconds. Without a usable clipboard the field is selected and a muted line says to copy it by hand. No "shown once" warning (that is SecretReveal).

无法自动复制：已选中，请按 Ctrl+C（⌘C）复制。
