TopBar

The compact class's one-row bar, in the place of the drawer menu bar (spec §4.6, §6.46; mobile layout spec §4.1): `layout.top-bar` (44, `row.roomy`) high, a `line-soft` bottom line, the page title (`body`, 600, one line with an ellipsis), and at the end search (the command palette) and the page's overflow menu, all buttons 40 × 40. A tab root (root) has no back button and starts the title on the 16 px content edge; a pushed page (pushed) leads with the back button, and the bottom navigation is hidden. The overflow menu (overflow) is the design Menu with 40 px items; a danger item sits last after a separator.
