MePanel

The Me tab's page body (spec §6.48; mobile layout spec §4.2): what the sidebar holds, drawn by the shell from the sidebar's data. The account row and the org switcher (which opens a bottom sheet), the 更多 group of every item the role sees (技能, 连接器, 定时任务, 设备, 插件, 扩展, 组织设置; 渠道 and 评测与运行 for org admins), 管理后台 for super admins, the account group (个人设置, 语言, 主题, 退出登录) and the system status line the compact class has no StatusBar for. Rows are `row.roomy` high, split by `line-soft`, in no box. Drawn in its page with the root top bar and the bottom navigation.
