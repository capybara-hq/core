ScheduleCalendar

The scheduled task runs of a week or a month (spec §6.40; scheduled tasks calendar design §4, §6, §7, §8). Week: Monday first, the 00–24 axis (12 hours visible, opened at 07:00), half-hour blocks with the state icon, time and name; three overlapping blocks side by side on Tuesday 09:00, four on Wednesday 14:00 drawn as two and 「+2」; the now line on Thursday 10:24; the dense lane of a collapsed hourly task (week-dense, with the admin scope). Month: 6 × 7 cells, muted outside 2026-11, three lines and 「+3 项」 on 11-05. Agenda: the phone's day buttons and today's 40 px rows. States: loading (skeleton blocks), empty (a panel empty state over the grid), failed (the load-failed line, the grid kept), selected (a line-strong border). Success is not colored. The copy is SCHEDULE_CALENDAR_FIXTURE's.
