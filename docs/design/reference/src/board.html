Board

A kanban board (spec §6.69; plugin full pages design §6): columns layout.board-column (280) wide, layout.board-gap (12) apart, each a lane on the hover ground with its head (title, count, the lock of a locked column) and its cards, one box each (panel, the line-card hairline, radius md); an empty column draws a dashed drop area. dragging: 「更新部署手册的回滚章节」 picked up with the keyboard, lifted in its place (raised, line-strong). over: held in 进行中, the column on the selected ground. locked-over: held in the locked 待审批, the card showing the column's hint. empty: the page empty state in place of the columns. loading: skeleton cards in the real columns. compact: the phone form, columns snapped side by side with the next one peeking in, each card with its Move to… menu button and no drag. The copy is BOARD_FIXTURE's.
