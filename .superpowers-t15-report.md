## Red proofs

Every new test was shown red before it was accepted:

- Scenarios 1–5: the fake `git` was made to skip `.mcp.json` / `.claude/commands/hello.md` and stop
  printing "Cloning into", the loopback server answered `Version: "0.0.0"`, the fake `gh` dropped the
  device URL line, and the local device's exec returned exit code 1. **6 of 7** acceptance tests
  failed (the seventh, scenario 2 sandbox, asserts the device's limits rather than the fixture, and is
  pinned by `mode: sandbox` / `network.kind: allowlist`). Restored byte-for-byte afterwards.
- Scenario 6: the scripted `sudo …` was replaced with `echo hi` and the outside write with a
  workspace write; both scheduled-task tests failed on the deny assertions.
- Review-before-launch in scenario 3 is pinned by the denied `rm -rf /` launch (no
  "Started background task", `review.decision: deny`) and by the successful launch's
  `review.rule: allow.git`. Review was deliberately not disabled for that test: the fake sandbox
  would then execute the command on the host.
