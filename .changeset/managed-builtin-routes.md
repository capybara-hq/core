Builtin tools are ready for the permission activation: once the one activation gate is on, Read,
Write, Edit, Glob, Grep, Deliver, foreground and background Bash, FetchAttachment and Skill enter
their device only on the managed route, recording the marked device paths before their callback
and running every provider call as a managed operation (a background command through the managed
launch). A call reviewed against another working root than the held device context fails as stale
before anything runs. A refused Full access (the old auto-mode codes and their full-access names)
is an actionable tool error. Managed operation results holding a NUL or a lone surrogate (Glob and
Grep output) are retained in chunks instead of failing in jsonb. With the gate off nothing changes.
