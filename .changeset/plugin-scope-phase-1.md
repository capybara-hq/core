Plugin scope: enterprise plugins have a scope (all, optional, named orgs), built-in and disabled states, and the org switch, requests (access, import, enable) and a scope sync worker replace the catalog policy, grants and the install-deployment step. Importing a plugin installs its extension; the admin plugin and extension pages and the workspace plugin and extension pages move accordingly.
